# NYC Restaurant Week Summer 2026 — Value Analysis
**Prepared Aug 1, 2026 · Ground truth: local extract of the official nyctourism.com listing API (648 participants, pulled Jul 31, 2026) · All own-menu prices read Aug 1, 2026 · Booking window: weekdays before Aug 16 · Base: the Bronx (Manhattan / accessible Brooklyn weighted)**

Grades: **[A]** read directly off the restaurant's current menu · **[B]** credible secondary · **[C]** journalism · **[D]** uncorroborated · **[inferred]** derived, derivation shown. "Gap" = comparable regular cost minus RW price, per head, food only, ++ (tax/tip) excluded throughout. Gap calculations are like-for-like on course count (2-course RW vs 2-course à la carte stack, etc.); the four comparisons that were not parallel as first computed are restated in the Course-parity audit below the value table. Method note: where a suspected trap turned out to be real value (S&W steak path, Morton's dinner, Tao dinner, Ocean Prime dinner), that is reported as the finding.

## 1. Bottom line — book these (9 clear the bar; not padded to 10)

1. **Casa Lever — $60 dinner (Mon–Fri only, thru Sep 6).** Largest verified gap: identical dishes à la carte = $107 (fluke $39 + skate $47 + tiramisù $21) → **save $47 (44%)** [A]. Landmark Lever House room. Books easily on Resy.
2. **Zuma — $60 dinner (book before Aug 16 — see conflicts).** Same-item build $116 → **save $56 (48%)** [A]; tenderloin +$15 still saves 43%. Easiest Bronx trip on the list (Grand Central). *Skip their $45 RW lunch — it's their everyday Ebisu price.*
3. **Hawksmoor — $60 two-course lunch (Mon–Fri, thru Sep 6).** Scallops $29 + 10oz strip $55 + fries $12 = $96 → **save $36 (38%)** [A]. "Same menu we always cook" — their words. Vaulted landmark room off Gramercy Park.
4. **Quality Meats — $60 two-course lunch (thru Aug 16).** Tuna tartare $32 + NY strip $72 = $104 → **save $44 (42%)** [A] — full-size steaks on an RW menu is rare. Steak or nothing: salad + chicken = $60 = zero savings.
5. **Café Boulud — $60 dinner (Mon–Thu 5–9pm, thru ~Aug 31).** Comparable à la carte $94 → **save $34 (36%)** [A]. Michelin one star (2nd consecutive year) [B]. Caveat: RW menu content verified from the listing extract, not yet posted on their own site.
6. **Frenchette — $60 lunch AND dinner (thru ~Aug 31).** À la carte $76–92 → **save $16–32 (21–35%)** [A], plus rotating **$60 wine bottles**. One of very few real *dinner* prix fixes at this quality tier.
7. **The Dining Room at Gramercy Tavern — $60 three-course lunch (7 days, thru Sep 6).** À la carte lunch $81, posted 3-course $78 → **save ~$18–21 (23–26%)** [A]. The real math: same one-star kitchen and room where dinner is a **$175** fixed menu [A] — RW is the sanctioned cheap door.
8. **The Bar Room at The Modern — $45 two-course / $60 three-course lunch (7 days, thru Sep 6).** Same dishes à la carte $64 (2-crs) / $82 (3-crs) → **save $19–22 (27–30%)** [A], inside a two-Michelin-star operation [A/B]. Walk-in bar/Terrace fallback if reservations vanish.
9. **Manhatta — $60 two-course lunch ($70 three-course, 7 days, thru Sep 6).** The honest label: **experience play, not value play** — regular 3-course lunch $78 [C] → savings only ~$8–18 (10–23%). You're buying the 60th-floor harbor view at lunch prices; dinner starts at $120 [A]. Infatuation top-13 RW pick [C].

**Near-misses (good, not top-tier):** Perry Street ($60 dinner, save 24–39% [A], Sietsema pick [C], easiest booking); Scarpetta dinner (save 23–36% [A], signature spaghetti included, ends Aug 16); Crown Shy dinner (save ~23% [A], one star); Ai Fiori (save 26–46% [A] depending on build, ends Aug 16, Mon–Fri only per own site).

## 3. Traps — where RW meets or exceeds ordering normally

- **Morton's $45 RW lunch** — their own year-round "2-Course Prime Lunch" is **$39** with the same core entrées and the same +$15 filet upcharge. You pay **$6 more during Restaurant Week** [A]. The cleanest trap in the set. (Dinner is fine.)
- **Ocean Prime $60 lunch** — like-for-like 3v3 the gap is only ~$10–12 (~17%): Caesar $19 + teriyaki salmon $39 = $58 for the first two courses, and the fixed cheesecake adds ~$12–14 of menu value. Dinner-tier pricing at noon, and the same $60 at lunch and dinner is the tell [A]. (Dinner snapper path is real value — ~35% off 3v3.)
- **Tao Uptown $30 lunch** — a rebadge of their standing Mon–Fri **$30 lunch prix fixe** (same starters/mains) [A]. Zero incremental value. (Dinner filet/sea-bass path is real.)
- **Zuma $45 lunch** — identical price to their everyday Ebisu 3-course weekday lunch [A]. Book dinner instead.
- **Estiatorio Milos $45 lunch** — their own menu confirms a standing weekday 3-course lunch PF; OpenTable diners peg it ≈$45 [C]. RW likely re-badges it, with the good stuff (whole fish) behind unpriced supplements [A structure].
- **Mr Chow $60 dinner** — publishes **no prices anywhere**, serves RW communal-style with a 4-dishes-per-course cap. Unbenchmarkable by design [A absence].
- **Quality Italian lunch** — Caesar + paccheri = $57 à la carte; RW **costs $3 more** unless you order carpaccio + truffle cacio e pepe [A].
- **Smith & Wollensky non-steak path** — chicken + salad = $48 à la carte vs $60 RW (**−$12**); also, the "prime rib" on offer is $29 roast-beef hash, not the $82 cut [A].
- **Benjamin lunch** — the entrée is a "**Junior**" sirloin, prices hidden on their own menus, and the cut you came for is +$39 at dinner [A/D].
- **Supplement erosion (general):** Lure (+$32 oyster/nigiri wipes out the entire gap), Crown Shy (+$10–32), Catch (+$15/+$20 + whole-table mandate + shared dessert), Oceana (+$5–10) [all A].

- **Gramercy Tavern $78 3-course** sits next to a stale "Winter Wines" label on an otherwise-current page — minor caution.

**Evidence runs out (no evidence found ≠ evidence of absence):**
- **Milos, Benjamin, Mr Chow, Empire** publish no usable prices on their own sites (Milos by-the-pound market pricing; Benjamin strips prices; Empire's menu is JS-unrenderable; Mr Chow has no menu page at all). Aggregator figures exist ([D]) but were not substituted per method.

- **Nougatine's famous cheap lunch prix fixe:** only pre-2026 sources; current menus show none. Treated as discontinued, not disproven.

**Confidence in the top-9:** High on 1–8 — every price pair is [A] same-menu arithmetic except Café Boulud's RW menu content ([A] extract + à la carte [A], menu-not-yet-posted caveat). Manhatta (#9) is deliberately included on experience, not math, with a [C] baseline. What would change the list: mid-program menu rotation (Nougatine/Perry Street alternate weekly; others may swap dishes), a Catch-style repricing at any listed restaurant, supplements added to printed menus, or the Sunday/end-date conflicts resolving against the listing. # Addendum 2 (Aug 1): Full-population sweep — all 600 remaining restaurants

Coverage: **306/600 usable comparables** (high confidence 263) — the rest publish no machine-readable prices. The 38 largest outliers on both tails were then deep-verified by hand from their own menus. Sweep data is in the `price_sweep` table; treat un-verified sweep numbers as [B/C] triage, not fact. Confirmed failure modes of the heuristic: it under-values expensive kitchens (mains capped at $70) and over-values casual formats by forcing a 3-course frame — both tails needed the human pass they got.
